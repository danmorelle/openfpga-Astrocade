Bally Astrocade core for the Analogue Pocket, ported from the MiSTer Astrocade core.

**Install:** unzip onto the root of your Pocket's SD card. Then put the 8 KB Astrocade BIOS in `Assets/astrocade/common/` named exactly `astrocade.rom` (rename it if it's called `astro.bin` or `astrocade.bin`; not included), and your games (`.bin`) in the same folder. If the screen stays black after loading a game, the BIOS name is wrong.

**Controls:** D-pad is the joystick, A is the trigger and L/R turn the knob. B/X/Y are keypad 1/2/3 and Start is keypad =. Hold Select for keypad 4 to 9 (X, Y, A, B, Up, Right), 0 (Down), CE (Left), - and + (L and R) and C (Start). A Dock controller's left stick turns the knob.

To pick a game, press its number, or pull the trigger, turn the knob to it and pull the trigger again. Reset in the core settings brings back the game menu.
